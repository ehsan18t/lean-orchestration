HCP Onboarding UI Revamp

HCP onboarding is now one guided workspace rather than a sequence of separate forms.

A persistent rail carries the three steps (Team, Coverage, Review) and expands Coverage into the provider's own coverage types, so the shape of the remaining work stays visible from anywhere in the flow. Every screen sits in a shared shell: breadcrumb and title above, a pinned action bar below, and one place for progress and problems to surface.

Configuring a coverage type opens in place as two tabs: choose and price the tiers you offer, then fill the benefit matrix of services against those tiers, each field bounded by the guardrails its template sets. The wizard resumes wherever the provider left it, and Review is the read-only account of everything before it goes for approval.
